💡 What Are We Comparing?

AM — Amplitude Modulation

In AM, the amplitude of the carrier changes according to the message signal. AM generally requires less bandwidth than the FM configuration used here.

FM — Frequency Modulation

In FM, the instantaneous frequency of the carrier changes according to the message signal while the carrier amplitude remains approximately constant.

🔬 About This Simulator

This project demonstrates the fundamentals of analog communication by generating, modulating, demodulating and analysing AM and FM signals. It also explores the effect of noise and compares the recovered signals using performance metrics such as MSE, RMSE and correlation.
